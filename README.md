This repository contains images from a prototype version of Magic Missile Wizard Duel (MMWD) along with diagrams and the implementation report for MMWD. MMDWD which was built by Ryan Vincoy, Roger Chiu, Hunter Olson, and Seth Traman for the Software Engineering course at UIC. Magic Missile Wizard Duel was designed by Annie You, Emily Mendoza, Kevin Cordero, and Nandana Sheri. The images of gameplay from MMWD are in the implementation report in the Project Deliverables section.

MMWD is an online player-vs-player video game where the players are wizards who cast spells to either attack or defend and the wizard who survives is the winner. For projectile spells, the player draws a path that they want the projectile to follow using their mouse. For spells that need to be placed, players will click on the area where they want that spell to be placed. To win, the only way to damage your opponent is the Magic Missile Projectile and to prevent projectiles from hitting you, the player can cast different spells to block or avoid the Magic Missile Projectile. Spells like the Shield or Wall of Stone spell can be casted to block damage and spells like Gust of Wind or Levitate can be used to stop the Magic Missile Projectile from hitting your wizard. There is a Mana system where a player will have to spend their Mana in order to cast a spell and the amount of Mana they currently have is indicated by the yellow bar on the top right of the screen. Slowly, Mana is recovered over time. Spells vary in the cost of Mana so the player must strategically cast spells when they are most needed and not "spam" cast spells where they are left without any Mana and are vulnerable to attacks.
